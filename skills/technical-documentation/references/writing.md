# Simplified Technical English

The writing rules in `SKILL.md` apply selected ASD-STE100 principles: short sentences, simple grammar, active voice, imperative instructions, consistent technical names, and one meaning per term. They are not a full implementation of ASD-STE100.

- A formal compliance claim needs the current specification, its controlled dictionary, and validation against it. Do not reproduce the dictionary or manual, and do not label ordinary simplified writing as compliant.
- For exact rules, consult the [ASD Simplified Technical English Maintenance Group](https://asd-ste100.org/) and its [official FAQ](https://asd-ste100.org/STE_faq.html). The site may block automated retrieval. If it does, say so rather than citing an unofficial copy as authoritative.
- ASD's [public introductory extract](https://docenti.unimc.it/f.raffi/teaching/2023/28609/files/file-da-scaricare-su-pc-e-portare-a-lezione-4-aprile-2), hosted by the University of Macerata, explains controlled terminology. It is historical Issue 7 material, not proof of current compliance.

A tooling exception to the Markdown rules belongs in the applicable profile, not in a silent rewrite of shared guidance.
